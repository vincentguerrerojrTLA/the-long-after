The Game

THE LONG AFTER is being built around exploration, survival systems, combat, stealth, looting, traversal and readable player decisions.

Explore

Move through urban, suburban, highway, industrial, waterway and Hill Country-inspired spaces.

Survive

Health, stamina, supplies, inventory weight and equipment shape moment-to-moment decisions.

Adapt

Combat, stealth, scavenging and movement are designed to work together as situations change.

Co-op readiness

The project is being designed with 1–4 player co-op readiness in mind; public details will follow verified implementation.
